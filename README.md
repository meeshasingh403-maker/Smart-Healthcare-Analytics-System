# 🏥 Smart Healthcare Analytics System | Power BI

### 📌 Project Description
- Developed an interactive **Smart Healthcare Analytics Dashboard** using Power BI to analyze hospital data and support better decision-making.
- Worked on understanding healthcare business problems and converting raw data into meaningful insights.
- Focused on patient management, doctor performance, appointment analysis, treatment costs and hospital financial performance.

### 🎯 Project Purpose
- To help hospital management understand patient trends and healthcare operations.
- To identify appointment and resource management inefficiencies.
- To monitor hospital revenue, billing and outstanding payments.
- To support data-driven decisions for improving hospital operations.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.
- DAX – KPI creation and analytical calculations.
- Microsoft Excel – Initial data inspection and validation.

### 📂 Data Source
- Kaggle – Hospital Management Dataset.
- Dataset includes Patients, Doctors, Appointments, Treatments and Billing records.

### ⭐ Features & Highlights
- **Business Problem:** Identified key challenges related to patient care, resource utilization, appointment management and hospital costs.
- **Goal of Dashboard:** Created five interactive dashboard pages to provide a complete view of hospital performance.
- **Key Visualizations:** Used KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Donut Charts, Line Charts, Column Charts and Matrix Visuals.
- **Patient Analysis:** Analyzed patient demographics, age groups, gender distribution and patient trends.
- **Doctor Performance:** Evaluated doctor workload, appointment status and specialization-wise performance.
- **Revenue Analysis:** Monitored billing amounts, payment status, collected revenue and outstanding payments.
- **Treatment Analysis:** Studied treatment demand, treatment distribution and average treatment costs.
- **Interactive Features:** Added slicers, filters, drill-through and dashboard navigation for better user experience.

### 💡 Business Impact & Insights
- Helps hospital administrators monitor important performance indicators in one place.
- Supports better understanding of patient demand and doctor workload.
- Provides visibility into billing and payment collection.
- Helps identify areas that may need operational improvements.
- Converts raw healthcare data into actionable business insights.
